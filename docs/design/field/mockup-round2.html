ClearWind: field mockup, round 2

The arrow is the hero: it takes as much of the field as it can. It is turned to your heading, so up is straight ahead, and it points the way the wind blows (a headwind arrow points down, at you). No headwind/tailwind words. The speed is the only text.

Examples: headwind 5 m/s (from 20° right), crosswind 3.3 m/s (from the left), tailwind 6.1 m/s.

← tap to preview the unit switch

Option A: arrow + number beside it

Arrow fills the full height. The speed sits to the side, big but clearly second.

Option B: arrow fills the field, number in the corner

Arrow as big as the field allows, centred. Speed small in the top-right corner, out of the arrow's way.

Arrow colour

Both options above colour the arrow by how the wind hits you (red-ish head, amber cross, green tail), an extra cue with no words. Alternative: plain black/white arrow, maximum contrast.

Switching m/s ↔ km/h

  • Tap the field to switch. The choice is remembered.
  • Taps on fields don't always reach the app on the Karoo, so also a remote button action "Switch wind units".
  • m/s shows one decimal under 10 (3.3), whole numbers above (12). km/h always whole numbers.
