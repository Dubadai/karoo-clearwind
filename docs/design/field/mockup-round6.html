ClearWind: field mockup, round 6

The direction arrow is always dead centre in the card, in every size. The speed sits to the right of it, centred up-down against the card and centred left-right in the space between the arrow and the card edge. "m/s" stays right under the number.

Examples: headwind 5 m/s (from 20° right), crosswind 3.3 m/s (from the left), quartering tailwind 4.2 m/s (from behind left), tailwind 6.1 m/s.
