ClearWind: field mockup, round 4

Built on style 2 (colour tile + arrow), made calmer: the full ring is gone. Only a thin arc remains, running from the front dot (you, straight ahead) to the small dot where the arrow points. The arc shows how far round the wind is from your nose. A tailwind has almost no arc, a headwind a half circle.

Examples: headwind 5 m/s (from 20° right), crosswind 3.3 m/s (from the left), quartering tailwind 4.2 m/s (from behind left), tailwind 6.1 m/s.

Layout 1: arrow left, speed centred in the space beside it

The speed number is centred in the free space, "m/s" centred right under it.

Layout 2: arrow always in the middle

The arrow sits in the centre of the field whatever its size. The speed is centred under it when there's room (¼ page), and centred to the right in short fields.
