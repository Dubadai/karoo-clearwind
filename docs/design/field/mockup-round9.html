ClearWind: field mockup, round 9

Calmer: plain white field (black in dark mode). Only the arrow has colour, and the colour means wind speed. The front dot, arc and end dot are solid greys (dots drawn over the line, so it never shows through); the speed is black, the same distance from the top and the right edge (12 px). In dark mode the greys flip and the speed turns white.

Examples: 1.8 m/s tailwind, 4.5 m/s crosswind from the left, 7.2 m/s quartering headwind, 11.6 m/s headwind.

Smooth speed scale

Colour slides from green through yellow and orange to red between 0 and 12 m/s. Shows small changes, but harder to name.
