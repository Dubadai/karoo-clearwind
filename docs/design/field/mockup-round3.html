ClearWind: field mockup, round 3

New arrow from your references: a thick rounded line with an open chevron head, no fill. Arrow first, speed beside it (layout A). Up = straight ahead; the arrow points the way the wind blows.

Examples: headwind 5 m/s (from 20° right), crosswind 3.3 m/s (from the left), tailwind 6.1 m/s.

Style 1: colour tile

Like your first reference. The whole field turns red / amber / green by how the wind hits you, with a white arrow. Readable from the corner of your eye; the colour alone tells you head, cross or tail.

Style 2: colour tile + ring

Like your third reference. A soft ring with a dot at the top that marks straight ahead, so it's obvious the arrow is relative to you. Costs a little arrow size.

Style 3: plain background, coloured arrow + ring

Like your second reference. Normal white/black field, the arrow carries the colour. Calmer on a busy ride page.
